# Changelog

## 2026.11.2 (not released)

- Brine and molten copper, iron and gold are real fluids on Forge and NeoForge (26.2 and 26.3): placeable, with still and flowing textures, a bucket and a liquid block each. Molten metals burn and spread fire like lava and never make new sources; brine behaves like water.
- Barrels, the crucible and the casting tray expose the loader's fluid handler, so pipes and tanks from other mods move their fluids. Buckets from any mod work with them. Dispensers place the buckets.
- Players on a dedicated server get the recipe lookup when they join and after every reload, so recipe viewers (BEI, JEI, REI) show every recipe and drop table there too.
- Every drive, hammer, crucible, casting tray and barrel sound is now triggered in game, and the pour stream particle shows while molten metal pours.
- Five drawn crystal textures replace the amethyst placeholder; crystals show under the fluid surface.

## 2026.11.1

First release.

### Interfaces

Changes to the frozen ids, registries, view records and data formats, newest first. Other workstreams follow this list.

- 2026-11-13: real fluids (additions, no id changes). `fluid/ModFluids` builds the four fluids of `Ids.FLUIDS`: source `primamateria:<id>`, flowing `primamateria:flowing_<id>`, liquid block `primamateria:<id>` (`PrimaLiquidBlock`; `ModContent.BLOCKS` still holds only the 12 blocks, `ModFluids.block(id)` gives the liquid blocks), a fluid type `primamateria:<id>` in the loader's fluid type registry, and the existing `<id>_bucket` items are now real `BucketItem`s (they empty to `minecraft:bucket`). The loader module supplies the fluid classes (`NeoForgeFluids`, `ForgeFluids`; `ModFluids` finds them by reflection): brine is water's fluid class and the molten metals are lava's, the metals are in the `minecraft:lava` fluid tag and brine in `minecraft:water`. Textures `block/<id>_still`, `block/<id>_flow` (animated) and `block/brine_overlay`; fluid models are registered by `NeoForgeFluidClient` and `ForgeFluidClient`. The barrels, crucible and casting tray offer the loader's fluid handler (NeoForge `Capabilities.Fluid.BLOCK`, Forge `FLUID_HANDLER`) over the pure `common/.../fluid/MachineTank` (`BarrelTank`, `CrucibleTank`, `TrayTank`, built by `FluidTanks.of(blockEntity)`); `BarrelState.restoreFluid` and `CrucibleState.restoreFluid` are new. `FluidIds` finds fluids and buckets by registry id (`carriedBy(stack)`, `bucketOf(fluidId)`) so other mods' buckets and fluids with recipes work; `FluidBuckets` stays the id table of our own and vanilla buckets. `CrucibleLogic.accepts(level, fluid)` is hot fluids plus any fluid with a casting recipe. `scripts/sync_versions.py` copies the new loader classes to 26.3. `scripts/make_fluid_textures.py` draws the textures.
- 2026-11-14: recipe lookup sync (additions only). `data/LookupWire` (common: gzip JSON, frames of at most 200,000 bytes, `Receiver`) and `data/LookupSync` (`send(player, sender)`, `receive(frame)`, `onDisconnect()`) carry `RecipeLookup` from a dedicated server to joining clients; NeoForge payload `primamateria:lookup` (`LookupPayload`, `playToClient`, optional) and Forge channel `primamateria:lookup` (`LookupMessage`). The server sends on every `OnDatapackSyncEvent` (join and reload) to players on a remote connection only; single player and LAN hosts share the in-JVM lookup. The client drops a received lookup on logout. The four Gradle builds take `-PrunDir=<folder>` to run the game in another folder than `run`.
- 2026-11-13: barrel added (additions, nothing renamed). Recipe types `primamateria:soaking`, `mixing`, `steeping`, `growing`, `composting` (records in `recipe/`, lookups in `recipe/BarrelRecipes`; the shared `recipe/PrimaRecipe` interface carries the grid-less defaults). Compost values are the data folder `primamateria/compost` (`data/CompostValues`, registered in `DataFolders.all`). Pure rules in `common/.../barrel/BarrelState` (+ `BarrelColors`); game side `block/BarrelBlock`, `blockentity/BarrelBlockEntity` (`state()`, `view()` gives a `BarrelView`), `barrel/BarrelLogic`. `ModContent.BARREL_BE` is the block entity type of both barrels, registered as `primamateria:barrel`. Fluids in barrels are internal ids and amounts: a full bucket (see `FluidBuckets`) pours 1000 mB, mixing recipes use the bucket's fluid. `steeping` has an optional `amount` (least fluid held, default 1000). `GameEvents.BARREL_FINISHED` fires with family `soaking`, `mixing`, `steeping`, `growing` or `composting`.
- 2026-11-13: void island start (additions only). Pure data in `common/.../world/` (`IslandPlan`, `StartMode`); game side `world/StartIslandPlacer` (called from both loaders' server started event) and `world/IslandData` (saved flag). World preset `primamateria:void_island` (empty flat Overworld, vanilla Nether and End) added to the vanilla `normal` preset tag. A world counts as a Void Island world when its Overworld is a flat generator with no layers. `scripts/sync_versions.py` now also copies the loader entry classes of 26.2 to 26.3 (they had drifted). `scripts/server_check.py` takes `PRIMA_LEVEL_TYPE` for a void-world pass.
- 2026-11-13: hammer feature (additions, nothing renamed). Recipe type `primamateria:crushing` (`recipe/CrushingRecipe`, lookup `PrimaRecipes.crushFor`), `item/HammerItem` (`tierOf(stack)`, block tag `primamateria:mineable/hammer`), `hammer/HammerLogic` (`crush(...)` rolls and fires `GameEvents.CRUSHED`; `onBreak(...)` is called by the loader break events), pure `common/.../crush/` (`CrushMath`, `AutoHammerState`), `AutoHammerBlock` and `AutoHammerBlockEntity` (implements `machine/Drivable`; `view()` gives an `AutoHammerView`), `ModContent.AUTO_HAMMER_BE`. `scripts/sync_versions.py` now also copies the loader entry classes from 26.2 to 26.3 (they are identical).
- 2026-11-13: drives added (additions, nothing renamed). `DriveBlock` (one class, windup, furnace and powered kinds, all with `StateProps.FACING`, `LIT` and `CHARGE`), `DriveBlockEntity` (`view()` gives a `DriveView`; `state()` is the pure `common/.../drive/DriveState`), block entity types `ModContent.WINDUP_DRIVE_BE`, `FURNACE_DRIVE_BE`, `POWERED_DRIVE_BE` and `ModContent.driveType(kind)`. A drive is a `WorldlyContainer`: slots 0 to 8 are the output buffer (the machine above delivers through the top; hoppers empty it through the bottom and sides), the furnace drive also has fuel slot 9 (sides only). A machine above implements `machine/Drivable`; a drive works it through `drive(level, 0)` and stops when `hasWork` is false, the buffer has fewer than 3 empty slots, there is no power or a redstone signal. The powered drive takes energy through each loader's energy capability (insert only). `SelfTest.loaderChecks` lets a loader's dev class add checks that run inside `SelfTest.run`.
- 2026-11-13: crucible and casting tray (additions, nothing renamed). Recipe types `primamateria:melting` (`input`, `result` = fluid id, `amount`) and `primamateria:casting` (`fluid`, `amount`, `result`, `time`), looked up with `CrucibleRecipes.meltFor` and `castFor`. Data folder `primamateria/heat` (`HeatSources`, `HeatRule`). `CrucibleView` and `TrayView` are filled by `CrucibleBlockEntity.view()` and `CastingTrayBlockEntity.view()`; `TrayView.Stage.FILLING` lasts `TrayState.FILL_TICKS` (10) ticks, then COOLING for the recipe time. `ModContent.CRUCIBLE_BE` and `TRAY_BE`. Fluids stay internal strings plus mB (`CrucibleState`); a hot bucket on a tray casts as many items as 1000 mB makes.
- 2026-11-13: sieve logic added (additions, nothing renamed). Pure logic in `common/.../sift/` (`SiftState`, `DropRoller`, `SiftMath`, `DropEntry`, `ResidueEntry`, `Drop`). Game side: recipe types `primamateria:sifting` and `primamateria:sifting_residue` (`recipe/PrimaRecipes.siftsFor` for lookups), mesh data loader (`data/Meshes`, `data/PrimaReload`), `SieveBlock`, `SieveBlockEntity` (`state()` and `view()` give a `SieveView`), `SieveLogic` (`interact`, `insert`, `shakeByDrive(level, sieve, minTicks)` for drives). `ModContent.SIEVE_BE` is the sieve's block entity type; `ModContent.registerBlockEntities` registers it. Hand shaking is one shake per 4 ticks (`SiftMath.HAND_MIN_TICKS` is the 3 tick guard). `RecipeLookup` is not filled yet.
- 2026-11-13: sieve rules. One block at a time, no storage: `SieveView.load` (0 to 4) becomes `SieveView.loaded` (boolean), `GameEvents.Sifted.load` is removed. Linked sieves: config keys `sieve.max_load` and `sieve.extra_block_factor` are replaced by `sieve.link` (true) and `sieve.link_radius` (1, range 1 to 3).
- 2026-11-13: molten metals are real fluids. `Ids.MELTS` and `Ids.DATA_MELT` removed; `Ids.FLUIDS` added (`brine`, `molten_copper`, `molten_iron`, `molten_gold`) and three bucket items (`molten_copper_bucket`, `molten_iron_bucket`, `molten_gold_bucket`). The `data/<ns>/primamateria/melt/` folder and `RecipeLookup.Melt` are removed. `primamateria:melting` has `result` as a plain fluid id, `primamateria:casting` has `fluid` in place of `melt`. `CrucibleView.isMelt` and `GameEvents.Melted.isMelt` are removed.
- 2026-11-13 (workstream C): new `anim.SieveViewSource` interface for the sieve block entity; `FILL` is `floor(load * (1 - progress))`; settings `client.animation_distance` and `client.sounds_per_tick`; `ModShapes` boxes for six models; sieve blockstate is multipart. Details in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 8.
- 2026-11-13: `copper_grains`, `iron_grains`, `gold_grains` renamed to `copper_dust`, `iron_dust`, `gold_dust`; `mineral_salt` renamed to `salt_dust` (`Ids.COPPER_DUST`, `IRON_DUST`, `GOLD_DUST`, `SALT_DUST`).
- 2026-11-13: `flux_drive` renamed to `powered_drive` (`Ids.POWERED_DRIVE`, `DriveKind.POWERED`, `StateProps.CHARGE`, config keys `drive.powered_*`).
- 2026-11-13: `stamp_mill` renamed to `auto_hammer` (`Ids.AUTO_HAMMER`, `AutoHammerView`, sound `auto_hammer.strike`, config key `auto_hammer.strokes_per_block`).
- 2026-11-13: block models use vanilla textures by reference; our own textures are 16x16 (`docs/ART.md`).
- 2026-11-13: scaffold. `Ids`, `ModContent`, view records, `StateProps`, `ModShapes`, `GameEvents`, `RecipeLookup` created.
